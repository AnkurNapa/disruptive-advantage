What plant teams ask us first

Contact Us

Does this replace our control system?

No. Control and execution stay exactly where they are. We sit above them and integrate sideways, so the platform runs on the estate you already have.

Where does our data live?

In your own Microsoft tenant and region, under your identity system, on your subscription. We do not take a copy out to run it somewhere else.

Do we have to replace the ERP first?

No. We read from it. Integration is one direction and nothing is written back to the systems that run the operation.

How long before we see something real?

The proof of concept is paid, tightly scoped and built on the production architecture, so it becomes the system rather than being thrown away.
