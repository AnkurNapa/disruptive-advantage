Beer

From mash to bright beer tank

A brewery's numbers move faster than its reporting. Wort gravity, fermentation curve, tank turns and extract loss all change during the batch, and most of them are reconciled only after it has shipped. This configuration tracks each brew against its intended profile while the brew is still running.

Questions it answers

Why did brew 4412 attenuate short? Where did this month's two percent of extract go? Can we fit another brew in before Friday?
Book a free demo

In the brewery configuration

  • Mash and wort profile against target
  • Live fermentation gravity, temperature and pressure
  • Attenuation tracking and diacetyl rest
  • Brewhouse efficiency per brew, not per month
  • Extract loss by stage, priced in money
  • Tank turns, cellar capacity and the brew schedule
  • Packaging yield and line loss
  • Excise and duty assembled from plant data

In the distillery configuration

  • Wash and low wines charge per run
  • Run profile and cut point consistency
  • Spirit yield per tonne of cereal
  • Continuous spirit account, still to warehouse
  • Cask inventory, fill and regauge
  • Warehouse position and rack location
  • Angels' share and maturation drift
  • Blend composition resolvable to the fill
Whisky

The spirit side, still to cask to blend

Spirit is accounted for by law and by volume, and most distilleries rebuild that account by hand every period. This configuration keeps it continuously, from wash charge through cut points to the fill, and keeps the cask position current rather than quarterly.

Questions it answers

What is in this blend, and which fills did it come from? Is the still running to profile tonight? What does the duty return say today, not at period end?

Wine

Intake to bottling, by vintage and by parcel

Wine data is organised by vintage and parcel rather than by shift, and the laboratory record usually sits apart from the tank it describes. This configuration joins them, so a blend can be resolved back to the fruit that made it and a recall scope is a query rather than an afternoon.

Questions it answers

Which parcels are actually in this blend? What is the recall scope on this bottling run? How is this vintage fermenting against the last one?
Book a free demo

In the winery configuration

  • Intake weights by parcel and block
  • Press cycles and free run separation
  • Fermentation by tank and by vintage
  • Laboratory results joined to the tank
  • Barrel and tank maturation history
  • Blend composition and component parcels
  • Additions, treatments and the compliance record
  • Bottling yield and dry goods reconciliation
