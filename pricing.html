Straight answers

The questions we get about money

Why publish anything at all?

Because the alternative wastes your time and ours. If the assessment fee is wrong for your size, you know that in ten seconds rather than after two calls and a slide deck.

Is the assessment fee really credited?

In full, against the first delivery invoice. It is not a deposit and it is not partially credited.

What if we stop after the assessment?

You keep the architecture document and the costed plan. They are written to be useful to whoever builds it, including your own team.

Do we need to buy Fabric before we start?

No. The assessment runs on a small capacity that can be paused, and we size the real one from what we find rather than guessing up front.

Do you resell Microsoft licences?

No. You buy them directly, at Microsoft's price, on your own subscription. We have no margin in your platform cost and no reason to oversize it.

Does the price change by country?

The scope drives the price, not your postcode. We deliver into India, Asia, Europe, the UK, the US, Canada, South Africa and Australia from the same two teams, and quote the same way for all of them.

Bring one week of your own plant data.

Before the assessment, before any fee. We will run it through the platform on a call and show you what it finds.
