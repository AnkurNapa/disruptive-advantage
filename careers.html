Working here

You will see the plant

Our engineers visit sites. You cannot model a fermentation you have never smelled, and the people who build here are expected to understand the operation, not only the schema.

Small teams, whole problems

You own something end to end rather than a ticket queue. That means design decisions are yours to argue for, and yours to live with.

Two bases, one team

North Sydney and Bangalore work as one delivery group with overlapping hours, not as a client team and a back office.

We build, we do not resell

Nobody here carries a vendor quota. What we recommend is what we would build, and we support it afterwards.
